How to interpret the ranking

The chart ranks the upper limit of filed rates, not expected personal offers, credit quality, or bank safety. Large rate ranges are especially important: the most attractive advertised yield may be available only under specialized conditions. Missing term bands are omitted; no extrapolation is made from other tenures.

Risk and return are different measurements. A high FDR rate may signal a funding need, but does not by itself prove weakness. A government-owned bank is not automatically risk-free.

No universal financial-strength score is invented here. A defensible ranking would require comparable bank-level NPL ratios, capital adequacy, liquidity ratios, profitability, governance, and credit-rating disclosures for the same reporting period.
