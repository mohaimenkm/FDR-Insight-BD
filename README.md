# FDR Insight BD — Smart Fixed Deposit Comparison & Analytics

A powerful, interactive Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR) comparison and financial analytics platform designed for Bangladesh. Compare interest rates across 40+ government-owned, private, and Islamic banks, calculate estimated investment returns, evaluate banking risks, and make smarter, data-driven financial decisions—all through a modern, user-friendly dashboard.

Here's your **FDR Insight BD — Smart Banking & Investment Analysis Platform!** 🎉

### Here's what's packed inside:

## 8 Powerful Features:

🏦 **Bank Explorer** — Explore 40+ Bangladeshi banks, categorized by government-owned, private conventional, and Islamic banks.

📊 **FDR Rate Comparison** — Compare published interest rates across different banks, deposit amounts, and maturity periods.

💰 **Smart FDR Calculator** — Enter your investment amount, select a bank and tenure, and instantly calculate estimated gross interest, tax deductions, net earnings, and maturity value.

🏆 **Bank Rankings** — Compare banks based on published FDR rates and identify competitive deposit opportunities.

🛡️ **Bank Risk Assessment** — Review financial-health indicators, risk flags, and important considerations before investing.

📈 **Interactive Analytics** — Explore visual comparisons, financial insights, and investment scenarios through a modern analytics dashboard.

🎯 **Investment Decision Support** — Evaluate potential returns alongside bank reputation, financial stability, and other factors to make informed decisions.

💾 **Export & Reporting** — Export comparison data to CSV, review calculations, and retain useful investment information.

# Data Sources & Disclaimer

FDR Insight BD uses published bank interest-rate information, including Bangladesh Bank data and available bank rate schedules.

**Important:** Embedded interest rates represent historical published snapshots, not guaranteed real-time quotations. Actual FDR rates may vary depending on the bank, deposit amount, tenure, customer category, and applicable conditions.

The platform is an independent financial information and decision-support tool. It is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by Bangladesh Bank or any commercial bank.

Users should verify current rates, applicable taxes, premature encashment conditions, and the financial standing of the selected bank before making an investment.
